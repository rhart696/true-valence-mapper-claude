# EXP-0001 — developmental pilot protocol draft

Currency: authoritative · Owner: Richard Hart / ProActive ReSolutions · Created/reviewed: 2026-10-05
Related: See START-HERE and registry.

Status: planned, not run. Proposed cohort: 3–4 facilitators, 8–12 executive clients, 15–25 uses across coaching, conflict, transitions and stakeholder contexts. These are feasibility planning ranges, not a power calculation. No recruitment or research data collection authorized by this document alone.

Prerequisites: release gates; sponsor-approved protocol; named pilot/privacy lead; consent materials, data store/access/retention, withdrawal process, jurisdiction/institutional research-ethics assessment where applicable. Participation and feedback must be voluntary and separate from employment appraisal and service consent. No client data in GitHub or development AI prompts.

Collect minimal pseudonymized participant/session/facilitator IDs, question and app version, context category, relationships count, time/completion/error/friction counts, selected actions and optional feedback. Do not collect raw relationship maps by default. Optional recordings/think-aloud require specific consent; identifiers and linkage key stored separately with restricted access.

Immediately after: fair representation; what became explicit; usefulness/intended action; friction; reuse/context; discomfort or adverse effect. Facilitator separately records technical defects and methodological surprises. Follow-up at 1–3 weeks asks what they actually did, what happened, alternative explanations and surprises.

Analysis: descriptive participant-level and session-level summaries; thematic coding with an auditable codebook, negative/deviant cases and facilitator comparison. Review interpretations with participants when feasible. Repeated sessions are not independent clients. Do not attribute causality or population efficacy; convenience sample, desirability, facilitator allegiance and context changes are limitations.

Weekly during active pilot: review defects, uncertainty, harms and evidence; record adopt/revise/reject decisions. Stop/pause if confidentiality fails, coercion/retaliation concern arises or significant harm is reported. Protocol/data retention and feasibility criteria must be finalized before enrollment. Output: contextual findings report with uncertainty and specific next decisions, not an NPS-driven success declaration.
