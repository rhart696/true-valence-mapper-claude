| Question | Initial method | Limits / next decision |
|---|---|---|
| RQ-0001 — construct | Cognitive interviews about conflict-specific wording, categories and inbound inference; compare broad variant explicitly | Identify ambiguity, not psychometric validity |
| RQ-0002 — externalization | Executive/facilitator accounts of what map adds to ordinary discussion | Self-report and facilitation/context confounds |
| RQ-0003 — asymmetry | Ask which differences led to useful inquiry and which did not | Do not preselect only dramatic maps |
| RQ-0004 — action | Compare burden/usefulness of structured prompts and action follow-up | Developmental learning, not efficacy |
| RQ-0005 — longitudinal | Re-map with context/question version and discuss changed expectations | Category change alone is not relational improvement |

EXP-0001 explores feasibility. Later controlled comparison/construct-validation study requires a separate question, design, sample rationale and ethics assessment. Literature map starts with source records and identified gaps; it is not a completed scoping/systematic review. Investigate organizational sense-making, interpersonal trust, question interpretation, confidence calibration, coaching interventions, visual externalization and power/cultural context with an explicit search/appraisal protocol.
