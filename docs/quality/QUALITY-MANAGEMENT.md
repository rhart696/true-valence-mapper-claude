# Proportionate quality management

Currency: authoritative · Owner: Richard Hart / ProActive ReSolutions · Created/reviewed: 2026-10-05
Related: See START-HERE and registry.

Lightweight gates: G0 relevance, G1 evidence/uncertainty, G2 decision/alternatives, G3 product/service/context design, G4 engineering assurance, G5 realistic pilot, G6 adoption based on findings. Apply proportionately; routine editorial correction does not require a full research protocol.

Work package: define user problem and relevant records; create/update requirement and material decision; choose acceptance and evaluation separately; implement on a branch; validate; update current state, change record, risks and unresolved questions. Qualified human approval applies to sensitive security/data changes and release. No direct production push.

Document currency: authoritative, superseded or archival. Lifecycle: proposed/accepted/specified/implemented/verified/planned/open/etc. Supersede records explicitly. Checkpoints identify commit, completed/pending work, tests, assumptions and next task. Standards mapping is scoped; public overviews cannot justify full conformity.

Operational evidence is aggregated/pseudonymized and separated from client records. Defect corrections and methodological changes have different validation needs; both update linked knowledge.
