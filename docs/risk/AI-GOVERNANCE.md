# AI development and future product governance

Currency: authoritative · Owner: Richard Hart / ProActive ReSolutions · Created/reviewed: 2026-10-05
Related: See START-HERE and registry.

Current use: AI assists repository analysis, documentation and proposed engineering. Product generative interpretation is deferred. Record agent/tool contribution, source verification, assumptions, changed paths and review outcomes in change manifests and validation notes. Use synthetic data; no client maps, privileged secrets or raw clinical/employment records in AI context.

Use NIST AI RMF governance and GenAI source-verification practices as inputs. ISO/IEC 42001 public overview provides adjacent management context, not a certification assertion. Existing AI_POLICY has unfilled vendor/retention/jurisdiction fields; these are unresolved, not established contractual facts.

Future product AI would need a separate decision/risk assessment, participant disclosure and consent, provider/privacy configuration, evaluation datasets, provenance labels and human review. Keep executive observation, executive inference, facilitator hypothesis, system-computed pattern and AI hypothesis distinct. No mental-state prediction, diagnosis or normative executive ranking.

The governance checker verifies links, not truth. AI output repetition cannot upgrade a claim's evidence status. Store concise auditable rationale and sources, not private internal chain-of-thought.
