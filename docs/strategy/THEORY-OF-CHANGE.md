# Provisional theory of change

Currency: authoritative · Owner: Richard Hart / ProActive ReSolutions · Created/reviewed: 2026-10-05
Related: See START-HERE and registry.

Programme hypothesis, not established causal evidence:

| Link | Proposed mechanism | Test / possible disconfirmation |
|---|---|---|
| Elicitation → explicit expectations | Writing ratings makes assumptions discussable | Cognitive interview finds categories ambiguous or no new articulation |
| Map → noticing | Visual arrangement may reveal directional differences | Executive notices no more than ordinary discussion; visualization distracts |
| Facilitated inquiry → alternatives | Evidence and uncertainty prompts challenge premature interpretations | Prompts reinforce assumptions or burden participants |
| Alternatives → experiment | A selected hypothesis supports a feasible interaction | Executive cannot identify an ethical/actionable test |
| Interaction → new information | Expectations compared with experience may update | No information, alternative explanations remain, adverse reaction |
| Revised expectations → changed behavior | Reflection may influence subsequent approach/coordination | No change or harmful action; context dominates |

Context/moderators: facilitator skill, power differences, employment conflict, culture, time pressure and relationship stakes. No requirement to confront another person. Record alternative explanations for improvement: ordinary coaching, prior relationships, events and reporting bias. Do not expose active-inference terminology as a client claim; conceptual connections remain research questions.
