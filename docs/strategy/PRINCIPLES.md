# Development and facilitation principles

Currency: authoritative · Owner: Richard Hart / ProActive ReSolutions · Created/reviewed: 2026-10-05
Related: See START-HERE and registry.

1. Evidence relevance and quality take priority over recency or volume; record uncertainty and counterevidence.
2. Preserve the difference between observation, interpretation, computed description and hypothesis.
3. Leader and facilitator interpret; software describes ratings without attributing mental states.
4. Collect minimum client information; default to pseudonyms and local handling for the pilot.
5. Make software and facilitation changes together with migration, export and accessibility consequences.
6. Keep history recoverable; one current-state entry point prevents repeated assertions becoming facts.
7. Scale governance to consequence. A typo needs a short change record; a construct or authorization change needs alternatives and validation.
8. Report what was actually inspected or tested and at which commit; never convert a plan into a completed result.
