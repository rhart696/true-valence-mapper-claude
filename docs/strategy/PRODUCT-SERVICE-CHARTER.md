# Product–service charter

Currency: authoritative · Owner: Richard Hart / ProActive ReSolutions · Created/reviewed: 2026-10-05
Related: See START-HERE and registry.

TVM helps leaders externalize, examine and test expectations about consequential workplace relationships through facilitated visual inquiry, and convert selected insights into safe relational experiments. Initial users: ProActive facilitators and executive clients in coaching, conflict, transitions and stakeholder contexts.

Scope: a reliable mapping interaction, explicit interpretation boundaries, local data handling, usable exports, optional sharing only after privacy/security verification, facilitator method and learning protocol. Preserve current v1 and useful prototype knowledge. EGPSD is an internal model, not an external standard.

Non-negotiable boundaries: one respondent's perceptions; reverse arrow is an inference; no diagnosis, psychometric validity claim, benchmarking or normative relationship score; source and hypothesis provenance visible. Pilot learning does not establish causal efficacy.

Out of scope now: product generative AI, multi-user collaboration, organizational dashboards, sophisticated network analysis and automatic intervention recommendations.

Accountability: Richard Hart is product/service sponsor and decision authority. Agents draft, implement and verify within authorized scope. Security/privacy reviewers and pilot lead must be named before client release; do not invent an existing committee. Material risk acceptance, live deployment and sensitive database migration require recorded human sign-off. Branch/PR preparation is authorized.

Success: usable facilitated sessions, accurate representation from the executive's perspective, explicit assumptions, feasible action, low conversational friction and transparent learning. Numerical targets are provisional; see SUCCESS-MEASURES. The operating cycle is discover, appraise evidence, frame uncertainty, decide, specify, build, verify, pilot, evaluate and update knowledge.
