# Executive journey

Currency: authoritative · Owner: Richard Hart / ProActive ReSolutions · Created/reviewed: 2026-10-05
Related: See START-HERE and registry.

| Moment | Executive choice | Facilitator/software support |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | Purpose, context, privacy and whether to participate | Clear current-perception boundaries |
| Map | Relevant labels and two directional judgments | Skip/unconsidered and accessible map alternative |
| Notice | Describe their own observations | Computed descriptions optional and secondary |
| Make sense | Evidence, interpretation, uncertainty, alternatives | Questions; no automated diagnosis |
| Choose/act | Priorities and a feasible experiment, or none | Desired shift, expectations, timing |
| Close | What to keep, export, clear or share | Disclosure matching verified behavior |
| Return | What happened; revise context and expectations | Follow-up and eventually snapshot comparison |

Research participation is optional and separate from the consulting service. The software must not require sensitive detail to move through the flow.
