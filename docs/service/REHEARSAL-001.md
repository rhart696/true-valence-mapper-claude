# First complete product–service rehearsal

Reviewed: 2026-10-05. Protocol version TVM-FAC-0.1; question version TVM-Q-1. REQ-0003/4/5/6/8/10/11. Synthetic walkthrough for engineering, not an executive pilot or validation of benefit.

Purpose: test whether a facilitator can help a participant externalize one consequential relationship misalignment, examine interpretations, choose a safe action and revisit what they learn. Session length and relationship count remain design hypotheses. Preserve the two conflict-specific questions in INTERVENTION-SPEC; do not substitute general trust questions.

| Step | Facilitation action | Product interaction | Observe/accept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare | Agree purpose, optional participation and confidentiality limits; use pseudonyms and synthetic examples | Open app; decide resume or fresh | No silent loss of an existing map; purpose and limits understood |
| Map | Ask exact directional questions; inward arrow represents the participant's inference | Enter three synthetic relationships; categories can remain unscored | Participant explains each direction without attributing measured motives |
| Notice | Ask “What do you notice?” before offering any computed description | Inspect map and one selected relationship | Participant can offer no pattern or challenge an interpretation |
| Examine | Separate what happened, interpretation, certainty and alternative accounts | Use current optional note; keep structured worksheet outside app pending field design | No unsupported diagnosis; uncertainty survives in the record |
| Act | Choose one feasible low-risk experiment or choose no action | Use action worksheet below; new app action fields are not implemented | Concrete action, stop condition and expected information; no pressured confrontation |
| Close | Confirm what to keep, erase or export; sharing remains unavailable for this rehearsal | Export if appropriate, reload/resume, explicitly start fresh | Storage guidance accurate; inspect report field inclusion and framing |
| Follow up | Ask what happened, what was learned and whether interpretation changed | Reopen retained map, review question/context consistency | Do not equate rating changes with measured relationship improvement |

Synthetic scenario: Executive A expects they would approach Colleague B about a problem, but is unsure B would approach them. One possible explanation is fear of consequences; another is an untested assumption about availability. These are fictional hypotheses. Safe proposed experiment: ask B whether there is a preferred channel for raising concerns, without assuming B has a concern. Stop/adapt if hierarchy, coercion, retaliation or safety makes this inappropriate.

Action worksheet: relationship pseudonym; observed event; current interpretation; alternative; uncertainty; desired shift; hypothesis; action or no-action rationale; expected information; what would surprise; planned timing; stop condition; follow-up observation; revised interpretation. Keep facilitator notes distinct and do not put participant records in the repository.

Facilitator consent language: “This map represents your current perceptions. Participation and notes are optional. You can pause, skip a relationship or decline an action. We will agree what to retain and who may see it. The inward arrow is your interpretation, not the other person's report.” Before real use, review this wording against the actual service agreement and data flow; no legal sufficiency claimed.

Rehearsal capture: build SHA, sanitized configuration, facilitator/observer roles, each task observed, ambiguity, friction, data handling, error recovery and unintended effects. Record absent capabilities as gaps, not facilitator workarounds that imply software completion. Pass a synthetic task only when witnessed; stakeholder usability and professional suitability remain unevaluated.
