# TVM intervention specification

Currency: authoritative · Owner: Richard Hart / ProActive ReSolutions · Created/reviewed: 2026-10-05
Related: See START-HERE and registry.

Provisional service protocol aligned to DEC-0003/4. Frame: 'We are mapping your current perceptions. This is not a rating of people or an objective measurement. The inward direction is your belief about their approach.' Agree purpose and relationship context, privacy and optional fields.

Select a manageable relevant set; 8–12 and 30–45 minutes are starting design assumptions, not optimal validated limits. Permit fewer. Exact initial questions:
- Outbound: How confident am I that I would go to this person if I had a problem with them?
- Inbound: How confident am I that this person would come to me if they had a problem with me?

Use High/Moderate/Low/Not yet considered with the executive's own explanation. Check whether 'would' and 'could' are being interpreted differently; record ambiguity for RQ-0001. Do not broaden to general consequential matters without a question-version record.

Notice: 'What do you notice?' Permit no pattern or an unexpected interpretation. Only then offer computed descriptions of the entered categories.

Make sense: What happened? What meaning are you giving it? How certain are you, especially about their perspective? What else could explain it? What might they say differently? What are the consequences? Distinguish evidence from interpretation; do not present prompts as diagnoses.

Act: choose one to three relationships if useful; specify desired shift, hypothesis, safe action, expected information, what would surprise, intended timing and follow-up. A conversation may be inappropriate where coercion, conflict, retaliation or safety concerns exist; adapt to context and professional judgment. Do not pressure disclosure or confrontation.

Close: executive reviews the representation and chosen actions, chooses what to retain/export/share, and gives optional feedback. Re-map with context and question version at follow-up; changes may reflect new understanding, not changed relationships.

Protocol variations and unintended effects become observations linked to decisions. Methodological evaluation remains provisional until pilot findings exist.
