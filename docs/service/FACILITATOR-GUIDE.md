# Facilitator guide — pilot draft

Currency: authoritative · Owner: Richard Hart / ProActive ReSolutions · Created/reviewed: 2026-10-05
Related: See START-HERE and registry.

Before: read framing and interpretation boundaries; confirm purpose, privacy and accessible alternative; use synthetic demonstrations. No sensitive cloud maps until release authorization. Agree operational versus optional research consent separately.

During: executive completes their view first. Ask what they notice; avoid interpreting color or position as objective strength. Inward arrow is executive inference. Explore selected evidence, interpretation, certainty and alternatives. Allow 'I do not know'. Optional prompts support dialogue rather than exhaustively scoring every relationship.

Pattern example: 'You rated your approach high and your belief about their approach low. What led you to those judgments?' Do not say 'They do not trust you'. Mutual low categories do not establish conflict; unconsidered is missing information.

Action: invite a small, feasible, ethically appropriate test and anticipated information. Record what would contradict the interpretation. Declining action is valid. Account for power and employment context; do not convert the map into employee appraisal.

After: executive chooses note inclusion/export/share. Capture technical friction separately from service learning; collect research feedback only in authorized mode. Follow up on behavior, surprises, context and harms. Discuss findings with consent and pseudonymized records.

Pilot feedback: fair representation? new articulation? intended change? conversational friction? reuse/context? discomfort/adverse effect? At 1–3 weeks: what actually changed, if anything? Do not promise improved trust or efficacy.
