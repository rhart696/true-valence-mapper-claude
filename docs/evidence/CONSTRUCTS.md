# Constructs and epistemic boundaries

Currency: authoritative · Owner: Richard Hart / ProActive ReSolutions · Created/reviewed: 2026-10-05
Related: See START-HERE and registry.

Perceived approach confidence is the proposed elicitation label, not a validated latent construct. Existing question is conflict-specific: expected approach when there is a problem with the other person. General ability to raise consequential concerns is a related but broader construct. Record question versions and investigate these differences.

| Representation | What it records | What cannot be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| Outbound category | Executive's current judgment about their approach | General trust, personality or objective relationship quality |
| Inbound category | Executive's belief about other's approach | Other person's actual belief or mental state |
| Confidence in inference | Executive's uncertainty about their judgment | Objective correctness or calibrated probability |
| Observation field | Executive's account of an event | Independently verified event unless separately corroborated |
| Interpretation field | Meaning supplied by executive/facilitator | Established causal explanation |
| Computed pattern | Difference/reciprocity in entered categories | Motive, diagnosis, network cluster or isolation |

Do not encode unconsidered as numeric zero in averages. H/M/L are ordinal elicitation categories; no interval arithmetic or normative composite. Psychological safety, trust and approachability are related research domains, not interchangeable labels. OECD guidance does not validate TVM.
